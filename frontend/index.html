Quick answers

Common questions when you get stuck.
Is my data private and safe?

Yes. Everything is stored locally in your own backend file (state.json). The app never stores broker passwords — only ownership and recovery details you choose to add. Profiles can be locked with a password.

Where does the live data come from?

Stock quotes & charts from Yahoo Finance, crypto from CoinGecko, FX rates from Frankfurter, Indian mutual-fund NAVs from MFAPI/AMFI-style public data, and news via Google News. If anything is offline, the dashboard falls back to demo data so it never breaks.

Can I manage my whole family?

Yes. Create a profile for each family member, give each its own holdings, property, cash and goals, then switch between them from the top-right profile menu. The dashboard re-scopes to the selected person.

Why are some markets shown as closed?

The sidebar Market Status checks each exchange's real trading hours, weekends and 2026 holidays. It shows which markets are open now and a live countdown to the next one opening.

How do I add an asset that search can't find?

On Portfolio (holdings) or Compare you can type the name, pick the type/market/currency and enter a price manually. The app will still track and value it.
